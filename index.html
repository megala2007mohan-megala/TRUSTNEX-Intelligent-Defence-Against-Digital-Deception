ABOUT & PRIVACY

Helpful context, without the false certainty

◎

About TRUSTNEX

A beginner-friendly awareness tool for individuals and small businesses. It uses transparent heuristics—not machine learning, a security certification, or a live threat-intelligence lookup.

▣

Privacy by default

Checks run in your browser. TRUSTNEX does not transmit or save the submitted URL, message or QR image. The dashboard stores only result category, score, check type and time in local browser storage.

◇

Know the limitations

A lower score does not mean content is safe. Heuristics can miss new scams or flag legitimate content. We do not verify sender identity, site reputation, page behavior or destination ownership.

Remember: Never share passwords, one-time passcodes or payment details from a link or message. When in doubt, contact the organization using a phone number or website you already trust.
